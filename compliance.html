Business model

Revenue designed inside the rules.

Fixed selling packages
Paid up front, never contingent on the sale — the exact line separating lawful assistance from unlicensed brokerage (ss. 1 and 125(2), C-73.2).
Professional subscriptions
Flat-rate subscriptions and advertising for brokers and professionals — not referral fees, which are prohibited residentially (Reg. r.1, s. 37). Built to the 2026 standard: online cancellation button, notice before trial conversion, all-in pricing (Bill 10; C-59 anti-drip-pricing).
Group-buying packages
Group-formation and tooling packages, fixed and not contingent on closing; legal structuring (indivision, direct member decision rights) before any launch.
Capital platform (regulated phase)
Placement revenue only via a registered partner dealer, then own registration — sequenced before any real dollar, never after.

Native advertising: never more than one ad in six, always identified — and advertised prices include every mandatory platform fee.

The capital path

Three phases to live investing — in that order, without exception.

Phase 1 · today
Full simulation
No registration required: fictional data, no offering-specific solicitation, permanent bilingual labeling, simulated KYC accepting no real data.
Phase 2 · launch
Registered partner dealer
Offerings carried by an exempt market dealer (or a 45-110 portal for small raises): French offering memorandum, independent appraisals (2023 rules), investor caps respected; dialogue opened via the AMF's fintech intake.
Phase 3 · scale
Own registration
Clé Capital registered as an exempt market dealer (CCO, regulatory capital, audited statements), via the CSA regulatory sandbox if useful.

"Why not addy's fate?" Two separate answers. Regulatory: addy operated unregistered from 2018 and was fined in 2025 — Clé sequences registration before the first dollar. Business model: addy became insolvent and wound down in 2026 — a distinct, business-model outcome — Clé's plan publishes its assumptions, promises no secondary liquidity, and routes placements through the exemption tiers that have real economics.

Funding & diligence

A $3.5M ask structured for the real Canadian ecosystem.
