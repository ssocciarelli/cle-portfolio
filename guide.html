Visitor guide · 5 minutes

Trying Clé, step by step — what you click, what you will see.

No install, no account required. Each step tells you what to do, then exactly what should appear — compare with the screenshots.

Open the demo → PDF version (printable)

Everything is fictional and labeled: people, properties, licences, investments. No real transactions, no funds, no real personal data. Reloading the page resets the demo — by design.

1

Open the demo

DOOpen ssocciarelli.github.io/cle-mvp — desktop or phone, French by default, EN toggle top right.

YOU'LL SEEThe landing page: three buttons, the demo network's live numbers, and the "fictional data" notice.

Short on time? "Visite guidée (2 min)" tours everything automatically — eight stops, no clicking needed.

2

Enter in one click

DOClick "Explorer avec un compte démo". Or go through "Se connecter": pick one of the three profiles; the simulated 2FA code is shown on screen: 123456.

YOU'LL SEEYou are in the feed, signed in as Marie-Claude Bélanger (investor). Three demo profiles exist: investor, broker, notary — each sees the product differently.

3

The network feed

DOPost something, like, comment. Spot the post labeled "Commandité" (sponsored).

YOU'LL SEEYour post appears at the top of the feed. Advertising is native but always labeled — never more than one in six.

4

The property market

DO"Marché" tab. Filter by region or price, then open "Triplex rénové — Plateau-Mont-Royal".

YOU'LL SEEEight approved listings, each with its property-type silhouette, and — beside them — a real Greater Montreal locator map: every listing is pinned in its true neighbourhood, and each pin opens its page.

Every listing carries an "advertising review" card: licence type, agency, seller disclosure, legal warranty — the disclosures brokerage regulation requires, enforced server-side.

On a phone, "Voir la carte" opens the same neighbourhood overview above the results — the caption says it honestly: overview only, listings open from the list.

5

The mortgage calculator — real Canadian math

DOOn the listing, scroll to the calculator. Change the down payment, the rate, or the payment frequency.

YOU'LL SEEThe estimated payment recalculates instantly — exact Canadian semi-annual compounding, not the US approximation.

6

A simulated offer opens a deal room

DOClick "Faire une offre simulée", pick a closing date, tick "I understand this is a simulation", submit.

YOU'LL SEEA deal room named after the property opens automatically: checklist, demo messaging, document statuses. The dialog is labeled "SIMULATION ONLY" — nothing here binds anyone.

7

Businesses under confidential dossier

DO"Entreprises" tab. Open a locked dossier, then sign the simulated NDA (your typed name is enough).

YOU'LL SEEBefore signing: never the business's real name. After: the full dossier, and every view is logged — the seller sees who looked. The French version of the agreement is presented first (Charter, s. 55).

The business list has its own locator map too — at the NEIGHBOURHOOD level only, never the exact address, and pins carry only the anonymous title: confidentiality holds all the way into the map.

8

Projects & Teams

DO"Projets" tab. Open "Cohabitat Brossard", apply to an open role, or record an investment intention.

YOU'LL SEEThe intentions thermometer moves. Every intention is explicitly NON-BINDING — no commitment, no funds.

9

The investor space — fully simulated

DO"Investir" tab. Open an offering, then try the data room: complete the SIMULATED verification with a FICTIONAL identity — no government number, document, or biometrics is ever requested.

YOU'LL SEEThe permanent "SIMULATION — NO REAL TRANSACTION" banner, returns always marked "not guaranteed", and a data-room gate enforced by the server — not by the screen.

10

Law 25 rights, for real

DOAccount menu (top right) → Paramètres → Confidentialité. Toggle a consent, request your data export.

YOU'LL SEEGranular, withdrawable consents, access and deletion requests, and an honest status for the features still awaiting production. The rights aren't a promise: they're implemented.

From that same menu, "Changer de compte démo" lets you experience the product as the broker or the notary.

Good to know
  • Reloading resets everything — the public demo keeps nothing, deliberately.
  • FR/EN in one click (header), dark theme follows your system, keyboard and screen readers supported.
  • Works on your phone — same address.
